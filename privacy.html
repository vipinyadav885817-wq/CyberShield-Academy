Privacy policy

Updated 21 November 2026

Reading without an account

You can read public lessons without signing in. Reading progress marked in the library is saved in local storage on the browser you use. It does not sync to another device unless you use an account feature.

Optional account features

If you create an account, authentication and related account features are handled through Supabase. Use a unique password and do not share it. Account-related data is used to provide sign-in and progress features.

Ads and analytics

Advertising is not active on this site yet. If ads or analytics are added, this policy will be updated to describe the providers and their data practices before those features are enabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository. Issues are public, so do not include passwords, payment details, or other private information.
